The Berlage is a small higher-education school liked to the Technical University of Delft which organises a two year long post-master studies in architecture and urban design. The long-standing first-semester course *Project NL* introduces the (often international) students to a research topic in the Netherlands (and, through it, to the Netherlands more at large) and asks them to reflect on planning questions through a mixture of investigation, argumentation and presentation, finalising in a digital and physical 'atlas' on the topic. Since 2022, tools from the <a href="projects/allmaps" class="internal">Allmaps</a> suite have been used in the research phase to georeference historical maps and contextualise the topic both spatially and temporally. 

I had only a small role in these courses, introducing students to Allmaps and participating in the discussion groups after each of the three phases of the 2024 (coastal landscape) and 2025 (mining developments) classes. I'm referencing the (digital versions of the) final presentations of these courses here, since they are a good example of what's Allmaps can make possible.
